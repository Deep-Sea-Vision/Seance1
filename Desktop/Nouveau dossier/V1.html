HOW TO PLAY

You are the central Artificial Intelligence of an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV). Your mission is to process sensor data (Cameras, Sonars, Magnetometers) to navigate the abyss.

The ocean is a hostile environment for an AI: total darkness, marine snow, false positives, and crushing pressure will try to trick your sensors.

Read the sensor data carefully and choose the best action. One wrong calculation could destroy the vessel.

DEPTH: 200M

The Light Problem

The YOLO AI algorithm works like a human eye: it needs light to recognize shapes and colors. Right now, the screen is pitch black. How should the AI proceed to find the target?

CRASH!

At 200m, sunlight does not exist. YOLO analyzes a 100% black image and detects "nothing". You crashed head-first into a giant coral reef. Mission Failed.

Marine Snow

The lights are on, but the water is full of floating sand and dead organic matter called "Marine Snow" (turbidity). How should you adjust your lights to see clearly without blinding the AI?

BACKSCATTER BLINDNESS!

The bright light reflects off the floating sand directly back into the camera, creating a white wall (like high-beam headlights in fog). Blinded, the ROV drifts into a fishing net. Mission Failed.

The False Positive

An object is floating ahead. The YOLO AI gives a 45% confidence score for "Jellyfish" and a 55% score for "Plastic Bag". Remember: YOLO looks for specific geometry. Jellyfish have smooth, perfectly round bells. Look at the sharp edges of the object. What is your final decision?

THRUSTER JAMMED!

The AI ignored it. As the ROV passed by, the plastic bag got sucked into the left propeller. The engine burns out. The AI was fooled by the movement. Mission Failed.

Camouflage & Geometry

You must find an underwater oil pipeline buried in the sand. Nature is chaotic and rarely makes perfect shapes. How does the AI know where the hidden pipeline is?

LOST IN THE ABYSS!

You followed the shadow of a manta ray for 3 miles. You lost track of the pipeline, and your battery died. Mission Failed.

MISSION SUCCESS!

Correct! Human engineering creates perfect straight lines and perfect circles. The YOLO AI successfully detects the straight geometry of the pipe under the sand.

Active vs Passive Sonar

Cameras are useless at this range. You must use Sonar. "Active Sonar" shouts a loud PING and listens to the echo. "Passive Sonar" only quietly listens to the ocean. You are in enemy territory. How do you find the target?

The Jamming Zone

WARNING! You sent an Active PING. The enemy heard you instantly! They deployed an acoustic jammer. Your sonar screen goes completely white with loud static noise. You are now blind. How do you track the enemy without your sonar?

TORPEDOED!

Visual range underwater is only 15 meters. The enemy fired a torpedo from 2 kilometers away using your camera lights as a beacon. Mission Failed.

PARTIAL SUCCESS!

You successfully used magnetic sensors to navigate through the acoustic jamming. It is too dangerous to fire a torpedo blindly, but you managed to record their acoustic signature and escape safely. The Navy is proud of your intel.

Biological Noise

The ocean is loud. You hear two sounds on your passive sonar: Click-click-wooo (random and changing) and Bzzzzzzz (constant and rhythmic). Which acoustic signature belongs to a machine?

The Whale Cover

You followed the random biological noise. It was a pod of sperm whales. But wait! Your short-range sensors detect a massive shadow directly under the whales. The clever enemy submarine was using the loud whale clicks to mask its engine noise! You accidentally found them, but you are now dangerously close (only 50 meters away).

CRUSHED!

Your AUV weighs 1 ton. The submarine weighs 5,000 tons. You smashed into their titanium hull like a mosquito on a windshield. You are destroyed, and the submarine didn't even feel it. Mission Failed.

STEALTH VICTORY!

You maneuvered silently and attached a magnetic tracker to their hull. They never noticed you. The surface fleet can now track their movements globally via satellite when they surface. Mission Accomplished.

The Decoy

The enemy knows you are following them! They drop an acoustic decoy: a chemical pill that creates a massive cloud of bubbles. Sonar waves bounce off bubbles like a solid wall. On your screen, you see two targets. Which one is the real submarine?

FOOLED BY BUBBLES!

You attacked the bubble cloud. It looks massive on sonar, but it's empty. The real enemy sneaks away while you shoot at bubbles. Mission Failed.

Magnetic Confirmation

You are 500 meters away. You need 100% confirmation before firing. The enemy submarine is made of 5,000 tons of steel. What final sensor do you use to confirm it's a military vessel?

HESITATION IS FATAL!

Temperature data is useless here. While your AI was trying to calculate water temperatures, the enemy submarine turned around and fired. Mission Failed.

MISSION SUCCESS!

Correct! 5,000 tons of steel creates a massive disruption in the Earth's magnetic field. The MAD sensor confirms it's metal, not a whale. Torpedo launched. Target destroyed.

The Design Flaw

You are descending to the Titanic. Most deep-sea subs are perfect spheres made of Titanium. The Titan is a cylinder made of Carbon Fiber (great for pulling tension, very bad for crushing forces). What is the main risk of using Carbon Fiber for deep dives?

FATAL ERROR

Carbon fiber is actually very light. Your AI failed to understand material physics. You descend at maximum speed, ignoring all safety protocols. The hull fractures immediately at 1500m. IMPLOSION. Mission Failed.

DEPTH: 3,200M

The Acoustic Warning

You are at 3,200m. The water pressure is 320 times heavier than on the surface. The AI's internal microphone detects a sound from the hull: POP... CRACK... What does this acoustic data mean?

CRITICAL NEGLIGENCE

You ignore the cracking and continue the dive. The micro-fractures connect to form a massive crack. IMPLOSION. Mission Failed.

DEPTH: 3,500M — RED ALERT

The Inevitable End

RED ALERT. The cracking sounds are continuous. The carbon fiber has lost 60% of its structural integrity. You are at 3,500m. As the AI, what is your emergency protocol?

CATASTROPHIC FAILURE

It doesn't matter what you choose. Pumps cannot fight 350 atmospheres of pressure. Ascending is too slow. The material fatigue is terminal. At this depth, the hull breach happens in 1 millisecond. Water enters at supersonic speed. The AI is destroyed before a human brain could even register the sound. The laws of physics cannot be bypassed by software. The ocean is unforgiving.
