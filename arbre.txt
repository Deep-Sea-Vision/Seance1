=== PATH B — MILITARY AUV ===
B1 (Active vs Passive Sonar)
Question: Cameras are useless at this range. "Active sonar" sends a loud PING and listens for the echo. "Passive sonar" only quietly listens to the ocean. You are in enemy territory. How do you search for the target?
→ Turn on active sonar (send a PING). → B1-ALT
→ Use passive sonar (listen only). → B2   

B1-ALT (The Jamming Zone)
Question: Warning: the enemy heard your PING instantly and deployed an acoustic jammer. Your sonar screen turns to static and you are effectively blind. How do you keep tracking the enemy?
→ Turn on the cameras to search visually. → B-FAIL-TORPEDO [FAIL: TORPEDOED]
→ Use the MAD (Magnetic Anomaly Detector) to follow their steel hull. → B-ALT-SUCCESS [SUCCESS: PARTIAL SUCCESS]   

B2 (Biological Noise)

→ The random click-click-wooo. → B2-ALT
→ The rhythmic bzzzzzzz. → B3   

B2-ALT (The Whale Cover)
Question: You followed the biological noise: it was a pod of sperm whales. But a short-range sensor picks up a massive metallic shadow directly beneath them. The enemy submarine was hiding its engine noise behind the whale clicks. You are now only 50 meters away.
→ Ram the submarine to disable its propeller. → B-FAIL-CRUSH [FAIL: CRUSHED]
→ Quietly attach a magnetic tracker to their hull. → B-STEALTH-SUCCESS [SUCCESS: STEALTH VICTORY!]   

B3 (The Decoy)

→ The smaller, noisy target. → B4   

B4 (Magnetic Confirmation)
Question: You are 500 meters away and need full confirmation before acting. The suspected vessel is made of thousands of tons of steel. What sensor gives you final proof it's a real submarine and not something else?

→ The MAD sensor (Magnetic Anomaly Detector). → B-SUCCESS [SUCCESS: MISSION SUCCESS!]
