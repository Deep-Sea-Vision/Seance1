=== BONUS RECOVERY SYSTEM ===
Rules: If a player chooses a "FAIL" answer, they do not restart immediately. Instead, they trigger the relevant Bonus Question. A correct answer acts as an emergency override, saving the ROV and allowing them to continue to the next main question. An incorrect answer results in a true Game Over.

BONUS 1 (Trigger: Failing A1, A2, or A3)
Emergency Reboot Sequence! The AI core crashed due to an environmental error. To reboot the acoustic navigation system, you must input the exact speed of sound in water.
→ Input: 340 m/s (Speed in air). → GAME OVER
→ Input: 1,500 m/s. → RECOVERY SUCCESS (Proceed to next question)

BONUS 2 (Trigger: Failing A4, A5, or A6)
System Override! The main camera is shattered. The AI is asking for a backup primary sensor to "see" the environment in 3D without light. Which hardware do you activate?
→ The LED Headlights. → GAME OVER
→ The Multi-beam Sonar. → RECOVERY SUCCESS (Proceed to next question)

BONUS 3 (Trigger: Failing A7, A8, or A9)
Critical Failure! The tether is dangerously tight. What is the absolute golden rule of ROV piloting when a cable is stuck?
→ Pull as hard as the winch allows. → GAME OVER
→ Give it slack and do not force it. → RECOVERY SUCCESS (Proceed to A10)
